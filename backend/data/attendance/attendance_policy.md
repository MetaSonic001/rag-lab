# Greenfield Institute of Technology — Student Attendance Policy

Academic Year 2025-26. Applies to all undergraduate programmes.

## 1. Purpose
Regular attendance is essential for learning. This policy explains the minimum attendance required to sit for end-semester examinations and the exceptions available.

## 2. Minimum attendance requirement
A student must attend at least **75%** of the classes conducted in each course (theory and laboratory counted separately) to be eligible for the end-semester examination in that course.

Attendance is calculated as: (classes attended ÷ classes conducted) × 100, rounded down to the nearest whole number. It is computed up to the last instructional day.

## 3. Laboratory courses
Laboratory sessions require **80%** attendance. A missed laboratory session must be made up in the scheduled make-up week; no more than 2 make-up sessions are allowed per semester.

## 4. Condonation of shortage
Students with attendance between **65% and 74%** may apply for condonation of shortage. Condonation is granted only for the following reasons:
- Medical illness supported by a certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 7 days of resuming classes.
- Death of an immediate family member.
- Participation in a university-level or national-level sport or cultural event.

A condonation fee of **Rs. 500 per course** applies. Students below 65% are **not eligible** for condonation under any circumstance and must repeat the course.

## 5. On-Duty (OD) leave
Students representing the institute in approved events receive OD leave, which is counted as present. OD must be approved by the Head of Department **before** the event and the OD form submitted within 3 working days after it. Internships arranged outside institute placement cells do not qualify for OD.

## 6. Late arrival
A student arriving more than 10 minutes after the class begins is marked absent for that period. Three late arrivals within a month are treated as one absence in the monthly report.

## 7. Semester-wise reporting
Attendance is published on the student portal every two weeks. Students with attendance below 75% at mid-semester receive a written warning and their parents/guardians are informed by email.

## 8. Examination eligibility and consequences
Students who fail to meet the minimum (and do not receive condonation) are detained in that course, marked "DT" (Detained) in the grade sheet, and must re-register for the course in the next offering. Detention does not affect eligibility for other courses.

## 9. Appeals
A student may appeal an attendance record within 5 working days of publication by submitting the Attendance Correction Form to the class advisor with supporting evidence. The Head of Department decides within 3 working days. The appeal decision is final.

## 10. Special provisions
Students with disabilities registered with the Accessibility Cell receive a reduced minimum of 65%. Students on maternity leave are given attendance relaxation equal to the leave period, up to 60 days.
