# Support escalation policy

The AI assistant answers from the published knowledge base. It must **escalate to a human agent** when the knowledge base does not contain the answer or when the case involves money at risk.

## Escalate immediately when
- The customer reports a duplicate debit or an unauthorised transaction.
- A payment is `captured` on RagPay but the bank shows no debit.
- A settlement is missing for more than 3 working days past the expected date.
- Account suspension, fraud flags or `RISK_BLOCKED` errors.
- Legal, tax-filing or regulatory questions.

## How to escalate
Open a ticket in Dashboard → Support with the payment id, order id, timestamp and screenshots. Priority tickets are answered in 4 business hours; standard tickets in 24 hours.

## Not covered by this knowledge base
Pricing negotiation, custom contracts, integrations with third-party platforms not documented here.
