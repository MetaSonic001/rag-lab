# KYC and merchant verification

You must pass KYC before you can accept live payments or receive settlements.

## Documents required
**Individual / sole proprietor:** PAN card, Aadhaar (for address), bank account proof (cancelled cheque or statement), a photo selfie.
**Registered company / LLP:** company PAN, certificate of incorporation, GST certificate (if turnover requires it), director PAN and Aadhaar, board resolution authorising the signatory, bank account proof in the company name.
**Partnership:** partnership deed plus partners' PAN and Aadhaar.

## Business website
You must supply a live website or app link with product pages, pricing, refund policy, terms and contact details. Missing policy pages are the most common reason for rejection.

## Timelines
Review takes 2–3 working days. Status: `submitted` → `under_review` → `needs_clarification` → `activated` | `rejected`. You have 15 days to answer a clarification request.

## Common rejections
Name mismatch between PAN and bank account, blurry documents, website under construction, restricted business category.
