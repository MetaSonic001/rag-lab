# Merchant FAQ

**Can I change the settlement bank account?** Yes, in Dashboard → Settings → Bank. A penny-drop verification of ₹1 is done and the change takes 24 hours. Settlements in between go to the old account.

**Why is my dashboard balance different from my bank?** The dashboard shows captured payments; the bank shows only settled ones. See Settlement reconciliation.

**Can I get a tax invoice?** Monthly, under Reports → Tax.

**Can I accept payments in test mode after going live?** Yes, toggle the mode switch; the keys are different.

**How do I close my account?** Ask support after all disputes are closed and the balance is settled.

**Do you support EMI?** Card EMI on select banks; enable it in Dashboard → Payment methods.
