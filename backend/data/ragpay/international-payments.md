# International payments

Accept cards issued outside India in 90+ currencies.

## Enable
International payments must be enabled after KYC. Additional documents: import-export code (IEC) and a declared business purpose.

## Pricing
3% platform fee plus 18% GST on the fee. A currency conversion markup of 2% applies when the customer pays in a different currency than your settlement currency.

## Settlement
International payments settle in INR at T+3 after conversion at the rate on the day of capture.

## Disputes
International cards have a higher chargeback rate. Respond to disputes within 7 days.
